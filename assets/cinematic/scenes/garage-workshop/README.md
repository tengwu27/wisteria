# Garage cinematic asset contract

## Geometry master

- Canvas: `1672×941`
- Camera: fixed 16:9 view from the open garage gate
- Day geometry master: `interior/sources/day-master.png`
- Night geometry master: `interior/sources/night-master.png`
- The night image is a registered lighting repaint of the day composition.

## Interior depth stack

1. Purpose-built exterior: a continuous Wisteria garden, café façade, awning,
   patio, path, and distant sky authored specifically for the window and door
   views
2. Rigid garage shell: gate frame, walls, exact window frames, mullions,
   sills, open garden-door leaf, threshold, workbench, motors, stool, tools,
   cabinets, lamps, and wall bicycles
3. Precisely isolated purple and black motorcycles

The side windows retain the exterior source's `1×` nearby-garden scale. Inside
the doorway zone (`x=520–790`, `y=180–630`), the café and patio are registered
at `0.70×` with a master-canvas offset of `x=150`, `y=15`. This places the café
in the garden mid-distance and raises the patio pavers into the lower doorway
so the threshold reads as a continuous approach rather than opening directly
at café-window height. The zone boundary remains behind opaque wall and frame
pixels at every parallax extreme.

The shell reconstructs only the floor concealed by the motorcycles. Its alpha
uses `interior/sources/day-apertures-keyed.png` only as an aperture guide;
all production shell RGB comes from the approved master and repair. Only the
actual left/right glazing and the open-air part of the garden doorway are
transparent. Frames, mullions, plants, door leaf, lower panels, sills, and
threshold remain opaque.

The motorcycle matte is extracted from the approved geometry master and reused
for the registered night artwork; it contains no floor, wall, window,
workbench, cabinet, or scenery pixels.

All browser plates are full-canvas registered assets generated by
`scripts/media/prepare_extended_parallax_scenes.py`.

## Transition depth stack

1. Repaired garage exterior
2. Independently authored near street lamp
3. Independently authored low left bush
4. Independently authored low right bush

`scripts/media/render_garage_parallax_reveal.py` keeps the garage structure
stable, applies a restrained push-forward, moves the lamp laterally, and lets
the low bushes part and dissolve. The same deterministic timeline is rendered
in reverse for the return-to-village sequence. The renderer uses fractional
pixel transforms, a gentle cosine ease, and 60 fps delivery so restrained scale
and lateral changes do not step or surge between frames. Foreground RGB comes
only from the approved transparent RGBA sources under
`transitions/garden-reveal/sources/authored/`; it is not clipped from the flattened
transition master. Superseded keyed inputs and pre-repair transition masters
are intentionally omitted because the accepted RGBA sources and clean
background plates are sufficient for deterministic regeneration.

## Generation brief

The accepted art was generated as an original Wisteria-world illustration:
hand-painted 1980s Japanese retro-futuristic manga energy, aged cream plaster,
teal enamel metalwork, brass piping, warm practical lamps, and cozy workshop
wear. The required content is two separate classic British-style motorcycles
(wisteria purple and black, without readable marks), two vertically hung
bicycles, a left workbench with opened electric motors and separated components,
an open garden door with a café-garden glimpse, and one window on each side.
