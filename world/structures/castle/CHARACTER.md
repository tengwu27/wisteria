# Castle character

The Castle is Wisteria's inhabited civic memory: protective without feeling
militaristic, ceremonious without becoming grandiose, and weathered by salt
air rather than neglect. Pale carved limestone, warm walnut, aged brass,
patinated teal metalwork, blue-and-white ceramics, mustard textiles, and
botanical ornament connect it to the village and coast.

## Gallery Room

The Gallery is an intimate salon rather than a neutral museum. Its warm
terracotta principal wall grows as a sparse, carefully tended collection of
paintings in aged walnut and brass frames. Dark walnut dado and ceiling, carved
limestone openings, small practical picture lights, and cool daylight from the
coast make the room feel inhabited. The registered exhibit region belongs to
the room. Frames, apertures, supports, and painting locations belong to each
approved scene composition and may be rearranged within that region.

The reference to Samuel F. B. Morse's *Gallery of the Louvre* governs salon
density, varied frame rhythm, warm collected atmosphere, and open-floor
hierarchy only. Do not copy its people, sculpture, paintings, or composition.

## Self-revealing art

An artwork has two representations with one identity:

- The wall proxy preserves coarse contour, value grouping, aspect ratio, and
  dominant color while accepting room lighting and distance.
- The inspector presents the canonical high-definition source without crop,
  stretch, repainting, or environmental color grading.

Every visible wall work must map one-to-one to a canonical Git source, Notion
catalog record, composition placement, exact aperture, and hotspot. Its frame
must match the source aspect ratio within 0.5%, remain inside the exhibit
region, and keep an aperture short side of at least 96 pixels on the 1672x941
master. There is no crop, stretch, or mat fallback. If those constraints cannot
be met, pause construction and request a different or larger placement.

Notion is the catalog and annotation surface, not a construction trigger.
`Ready` never generates or places self-revealing art. An explicit Codex request
first creates a temporary standalone painting and an updated room candidate.
Only visual approval permits a new composition version, persisted media, a
draft construction PR, or a Notion `Processing` upsert.

Existing canonical paintings may move but must not be repainted. Each new
composition begins from the latest approved scene and all hash-verified
canonical works. Empty linen-backed supports and non-art ornament may remain
when useful. Retain the aged-walnut and brass treatment unless the curation
brief explicitly requests another treatment.
